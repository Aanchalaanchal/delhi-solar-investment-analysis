# Contractor request for quotation — four-family HVAC and rooftop PV

**Issue:** Draft for supplier pricing. **Location:** Vasant Vihar neighborhood, New Delhi (exact property address provided privately to bidders only). **Status:** Planning assumptions, not an approved MEP design.

## Project brief

Four independent family floors; one **proposed** combined BRPL electricity connection, subject to utility approval. Each floor is provisionally **2,500 sq ft of conditioned space** (10,000 sq ft total). Preliminary HVAC planning capacity **40 TR total**, with contractor to independently verify design load using room-by-room sensible/latent heat gain calculations and proposed ventilation design. Do not quote 40 TR as a final engineering requirement without verification.

Furnished roof: assume **100 m² usable PV area**, approximately **10 kW** using 10 m²/kW. Optional elevated canopy: 40 m² footprint, 75% panel coverage, about **3 kW** incremental; alternate terrace: 140 m² usable, about **14 kW**. These are not surveyed areas.

## HVAC quote packages

Submit separately priced bids for:
1. **VRF/VRV**: four independently controllable floor zones; room-level control; specify indoor/outdoor unit model numbers, rated and seasonal cooling efficiencies, outdoor design temperature derating, total electrical input kW, fresh air, refrigerant concentration/safety checks, pipe lengths, redundancy and acoustic impact.
2. **High-efficiency inverter ducted splits**: per-floor zoning, room-by-room dampers or dedicated units, actual part-load control, fresh-air provision, total indoor/outdoor fan input, duct insulation and balancing.
3. **Air-cooled chiller**: central plant space, distribution pumps, fan coils/AHUs, electrical consumption including auxiliaries, redundancy, maintenance and water/condensate provisions.

For all: attach a room-by-room cooling-load schedule (sensible and latent), 24-hour peak diversified demand calculation, conditioned-space assumptions, Delhi summer design condition, commissioning checklist, detailed BOQ, equipment warranties, and annual maintenance contract.

## PV quote packages

Quote **10 kW furnished roof**, **10 + 3 kW with engineered elevated canopy**, and **14 kW alternate terrace** separately. Include measured usable areas, obstruction/shadow plan, annual hourly yield estimate, modules/inverter model, DC/AC ratio, mounting and waterproofing, wind/seismic structural certification, fire access, earthing, surge protection, monitoring, net-metering/BRPL application support, O&M, performance guarantees, degradation warranties and a line-item tax-inclusive quote.

**Do not presume subsidies or a specific net-metering export tariff.** Require supplier to cite the applicable 2026 DERC/Delhi solar-policy amendments and BRPL connection rules.

## Required supplier response columns

Supplier; quote date; HVAC/PV package; measured area; design TR or DC kWp; electrical input kW; expected annual kWh; installed price incl taxes; exclusions; annual AMC; warranty; efficiency evidence; design approvals; lead time; payment milestones; performance guarantee; exceptions to specification.

## Bid evaluation (suggested)

- Engineering completeness and safety: **30%**
- 15-year lifecycle cost (installed cost + modeled energy + AMC + major replacements): **30%**
- Part-load efficiency and independent floor control: **20%**
- Serviceability, warranties and local support: **15%**
- Delivery and implementation schedule: **5%**

**Gate:** no equipment purchase until room-by-room HVAC design, roof structural survey, sanctioned-load review and BRPL interconnection path are validated.
