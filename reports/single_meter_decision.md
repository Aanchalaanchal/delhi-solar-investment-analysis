# Current design decision: one combined BRPL electricity meter

**Supersedes the earlier four- and five-meter proposals.** The new four-family residence is proposed to have one BRPL electricity connection for all private floors, central AC, lifts, pumps and common services.

- Solar configuration to evaluate: one building-wide rooftop PV system, primarily via conventional net metering, subject to BRPL's written eligibility and sanctioned-load confirmation.
- Electrical forecast: four families' non-AC demand plus one central AC plant plus other common loads, all aggregated on the same connection.
- Historical reference: the existing building's combined electricity expenditure of ₹80,000–₹1,00,000 per month. It is **not** the proposed building's consumption forecast.
- Weather basis: NASA POWER 2023–2025 PV-model mean ~1,414.73 kWh/kW-year under current assumptions.
- Compare the furnished Final Plan regular roof, its elevated canopy variant, and the more open A11_8VV alternative using the *proposed* building's forecast kWh.
- Do not use earlier per-account VNM allocation outputs for the current design. Old exploratory source modules remain in the repository for historical reference only, but the active dashboard sections were retired.
- Critical approvals: BRPL single connection for the proposed property, sanctioned load, rooftop net-metering application and permissible capacity, structural/wind review of elevated canopy, and a detailed HVAC load study.
- Financial limitation: annual production-offset proxy; no hourly load matching, export credits, actual tariff slabs, subsidies, or confirmed installer quotes.

This is a proposed metering design, not a statement that BRPL has approved it.
